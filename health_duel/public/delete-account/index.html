Delete your account

Last updated August 18, 2026

Deleting your account is permanent and removes your data from Health Duel. Here's exactly what happens.

From the app

  1. Open Health Duel and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Delete Account.
  3. Re-enter your credentials to confirm it's really you.
  4. Confirm deletion. This takes effect immediately and can't be undone.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile — display name, photo, streak, and account record.
  • Your friends list and challenge history.
  • Health Connect access — Health Duel's permission to read your step count is revoked.
  • Your Google sign-in link to Health Duel.

What's retained

Duel history your opponents can still see

If you completed duels with someone else, that record stays on their account so their history isn't broken by your deletion — but it's anonymized: your name and photo are replaced with a generic "deleted user" marker, and it can no longer be traced back to your identity or your data.

Can't access the app?

If you no longer have the app installed or can't sign in — lost phone, old account — you can request deletion manually. We'll verify you're the account owner before removing anything.
