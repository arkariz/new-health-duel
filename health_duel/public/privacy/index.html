Privacy Policy

Last updated August 18, 2026

Health Duel is a 24-hour step-count competition app. This page explains, in plain terms, what we collect, who can see it, and how to remove it.

What we collect

  • Account info — your display name and profile photo, set when you sign in with Google.
  • Step count — read from Health Connect (READ_STEPS only — we never request write access to your health data).
  • Duel & challenge activity — the challenges and duels you start or join, your streak, and your friends list.

We don't collect your email address in our database, your location, or any health data beyond step count — no heart rate, no distance, no calories.

Who sees it

Duel opponents

When you're in an active duel, the other participant can see your name, profile photo, and live step count for the duration of that duel. That's the entire point of a duel — it's not a bug, but it is a deliberate trade-off you should know about.

We don't sell your data, and we don't share it with advertisers — Health Duel has no ads and no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

Being honest about trust

Health Duel has no server-side anti-cheat. Our security rules stop your opponent from editing your step count — they can't touch your value, only their own. They do not stop you from inflating your own number by manually adding steps in Health Connect. Duels and streaks are trust-based, meant for people who already know each other — coworkers, friends, couples — not a verified competitive leaderboard.

How long we keep it

Your account data stays until you delete your account. Completed duels remain visible to the other participant afterward (so their history isn't broken by your deletion) but are disconnected from your identity once you delete your account — see Delete your account for exactly what's removed.

Your choices

  • Revoke Health Connect permission at any time from Android Settings → Health Connect → App permissions.
  • Delete your account and all associated data in-app, from Settings → Delete Account. Details: Delete your account.
